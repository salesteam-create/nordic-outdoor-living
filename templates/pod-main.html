Which POD is right for me?

Premium is the compact POD with a short shell and manual roof. Signature adds a full-height shell, motorized roof, control module, LED lighting and a storage cabinet. Exclusive adds a second cabinet, Bluetooth speakers, a fan and an infrared heater.

Can I swap the BBQ for something else?

Yes. The BBQ cabinet takes one cooking appliance at a time: the stainless BBQ, a smoker, a pizza oven or a griddle. They are interchangeable.

What utilities does it need?

One 110 V / 20 A circuit (a 20 ft cord is included), propane or natural gas, 1/2 in PEX hot and cold water, and a 2 in grey water line to the included tank or a drain. Everything connects at one rear panel.

How is it delivered?

Your POD ships flat-packed on a custom pallet (about 10 ft long and 30 in wide) to your driveway, with appliances boxed separately. [Assembly and delivery terms]

Do I need HOA approval or a permit?

It depends on your HOA and local rules. We provide an HOA approval pack with the spec sheet, dimensions and renders so the request is simple. Check permit requirements with your city or county.
