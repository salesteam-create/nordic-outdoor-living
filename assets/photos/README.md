# Lifestyle photos

Drop photos here with these exact names and they appear on the homepage automatically:

- `build.jpg`: "Skip the build" section. Close view of the open kitchen (grill, counter, storage). Landscape, at least 1400px wide. Falls back to the render.
- `life-1.jpg`: family and friends around the open pod, warm evening light.
- `life-2.jpg`: pod closed beside a pool, clean architectural view.
- `life-3.jpg`: one cook, counter in use, gate half open.

The three "Life with a pod" cards are cropped to roughly 4:5, so portrait or square shots work best (at least 1200px on the short side). Until a photo is added, the card shows a "Photo coming" placeholder.

Keep each file under about 400 KB (JPG at roughly 75 to 80% quality).

The hero uses the background video in `assets/video/` instead of a photo.
