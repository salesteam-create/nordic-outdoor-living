    Which pod is right for me?

    Good is the compact option with manual opening. Better adds full height, electronic opening, LED lighting and an exhaust fan. Best adds more length, a fourth cabinet and Bluetooth speakers.

    Do I need HOA approval or a permit?

    It depends on your HOA and local rules. We provide an HOA approval pack with the spec sheet, dimensions and renders so the request is simple. Check permit requirements with your city or county.
