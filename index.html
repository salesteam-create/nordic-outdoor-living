Why a pod

Skip the build. Keep the kitchen.

Most outdoor kitchens are a project: a designer, a contractor, masonry, appliances ordered one by one, and weeks of work in your yard.

The pod arrives as one finished structure with the roof, appliances and storage already in place. You choose a finish, check that it fits, and get ready for delivery.

    What is the NOL pod?

    A modular outdoor kitchen with a roof. The grill, sink, fridge, prep counter and storage are built into one structure with a gate that opens when you cook and closes when you're done.

    Do I need HOA approval or a permit?

    It depends on your HOA and local rules. We provide an HOA approval pack with the spec sheet, dimensions and renders so the request is simple. Check permit requirements with your city or county.

    What utilities does it need?

    Power, gas and water requirements are listed on the spec sheet and in the delivery and site guide. [Voltage, fuel type, water and drainage]

    How is it delivered?

    The pod is delivered as one finished structure. Coverage, lead time and setup are listed in the delivery and site guide. [Delivery details]

    Can I pay monthly?

    Yes. Financing is offered at checkout, subject to approval. [Provider and example terms]

    Is the reservation deposit refundable?

    Your deposit counts toward the price of your pod. Refund terms are shown before you pay. [Refund terms]
