Description

The NOL POD is a complete outdoor kitchen in one architectural shell. A sink, a stainless BBQ and a glass-door fridge sit under a roof that lifts by hand into a canopy when you cook, then closes over everything when you're done. It arrives flat-packed and connects to your home at a single rear panel.

    Why we built it

    An outdoor kitchen shouldn't need a building permit and a summer of trades.

    Most outdoor kitchens are a construction project: masonry, a plumber, an electrician and weeks of mess. Then they sit open to the weather all winter.

    The POD is one finished piece. It arrives ready to connect, cooks like a real kitchen, and closes into a clean, weather-tight shape when you're done.

    How it opens

    One roof. Two lives.

    1. ClosedA clean, weather-tight shape in the garden.
    2. LiftThe roof swings up on gas struts, by hand.
    3. CookIt becomes a canopy over the BBQ, sink and fridge.

    FAQ

    What's included with the POD?

    The shell with a lift-up roof, a sink cabinet with black faucet, plumbing kit and grey water tank, a BBQ cabinet with a stainless BBQ, a glass-door fridge, and a countertop over the fridge.

    Can I swap the BBQ for something else?

    Yes. The BBQ cabinet takes one cooking appliance: the stainless BBQ, a griddle, a pizza oven or a pellet smoker. Choose it when you reserve.

    What utilities does it need?

    One 110 V / 20 A circuit (a 20 ft cord is included), propane or natural gas, 1/2 in PEX hot and cold water, and a 2 in grey water line to the included tank or a drain. Everything connects at one rear panel.

    How much space do I need?

    The POD is about 10 ft wide, 7.5 ft high and 4 ft deep. Leave about 3 ft clear in front to open the roof and cook, and about 3 ft of access to bring the pallet in.

    How is it delivered?

    Flat-packed on a custom pallet, about 10 ft long and 30 in wide, to your driveway. Appliances are boxed separately. [Assembly and delivery terms to confirm]

    Do I need HOA approval or a permit?

    It depends on your HOA and local rules. We provide an approval pack with the spec sheet, dimensions and renders. Check permit requirements with your city or county.

    How does financing work?

    Reserve with a deposit, then spread the rest over monthly payments from /month, subject to approval.

    Is the deposit refundable?

    Your deposit counts toward the price of your POD. Refund terms are shown before you pay. [Refund terms to confirm]

    Which colours can I choose?

    Cabinet faces, exterior panels and interior panels each come in white or grey. The frame, cabinet sides, faucet and handles are always black.
